# Round 1 seal (2026-09-10)

Preregistered config completed: LLaDA-8B-Instruct, GSM8K test[0:50], gen 128, 64 steps,
block 32, 5 deltas, k in {0,4,...,32} x sources {aligned, final, first}, resume t0 in
{8,16,32,48}. 6570 records (50 old, 224 full recompute, 5600 reuse, 696 resume).
name_edit exists for 24/50 prompts (single-token name required). q0-q3 computed on CPU,
q4-q49 on GPU; the self-injection control passed (token agreement 1.000, states
bit-identical), so all numbers are interpretable. Full tables with 95% bootstrap CIs:
`results/pilot_gpu/ROUND1_SEAL.md` (generated by `seal_round1.py`). Figures: fig1-4 in
`results/pilot_gpu/`.

Primary metric: answer accuracy vs gold, paired against full recompute of the same P+D
("quality loss", pp). Secondary: answer agreement with full recompute; generated-token
agreement is descriptive only.

## Reference numbers

| | acc |
|---|---|
| old request P | 0.64 [0.50, 0.78] |
| P+irrelevant_append (full) | 0.68 |
| P+hint_append (full) | 0.96 |
| P+multiturn_append (full) | 0.64 |
| P+name_edit (full, n=24) | 0.62 |

An irrelevant one-sentence append changes the final numeric answer in 36% of prompts
(answer same as old = 0.64) while leaving accuracy unchanged. The decoding trajectory is
highly sensitive to context; accuracy is not.

## C1 and C2 hold at n=50

- C1: at k=4 the reused states have cos-sim 0.999 to the recomputed ones, yet
  generated-token agreement with full recompute is 0.73 [0.68, 0.78] (pooled preserving,
  aligned). Numerically near-identical state does not give the same trajectory.
- C2: at k=16 token agreement is 0.50 [0.45, 0.56] while quality loss is
  +0.0 pp [-5.2, +5.2]. Different trajectory, same task quality.

## Exp1 / Exp4: drift is layer-monotone and shrinks late in denoising

Mean cos-sim at shared positions, layers 0/8/16/24/32: appends and edits
1.000 / 0.998 / 0.992 / 0.97 / 0.92-0.94; multiturn 1.000 / 0.997 / 0.980 / 0.926 / 0.873.
Semantic relevance matters but is second-order (hint 0.920 vs irrelevant 0.938 at L32);
delta size (multiturn, ~100 new tokens) is first-order.

Step direction (Fig4): multiturn deep-layer drift falls monotonically 0.13 -> 0.055 over the
64 steps, confirming the n=5 signal. The other deltas show a hump: drift rises through
mid-generation and falls in the last ~15 steps. Late-step state is the most reusable for
every delta.

## Exp3: spatial locality is dead

Per-prompt Pearson r between distance-to-change and deep-layer drift: irrelevant -0.05,
hint -0.06, name_edit +0.13, number_edit +0.22, multiturn -0.28 (driven entirely by the ~5
tokens adjacent to the change; flat beyond). Fraction of prompts with r < -0.3 is 0.00 for
all deltas except multiturn (0.28). Same-length edits show drift *increasing* with distance.
Bidirectional attention spreads the update globally; distance-based invalidation is not a
viable mechanism. Branch closed.

## Exp2: layer-state reuse

Pooled over answer-preserving deltas (n=174 pairs), quality loss in pp vs full recompute:

| source | k=4 | k=8 | k=12 | k=16 | k=20 | k=24 | k=28 | k=32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| aligned | -2.3 | -0.6 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.0 | -2.9 | -3.4 | -5.2 |
| first (step-0 snapshot) | -0.6 | +4.6 | +3.4 | 0.0 | +1.7 | +2.9 | +1.7 | +0.6 |
| final (last-step snapshot) | +3.4 | -1.1 | 0.0 | -0.6 | -8.0 | -12.6 | -15.5 | -13.8 |
| compute saved | 3% | 7% | 10% | 14% | 17% | 21% | 24% | 28% |

CI half-width is about 5 pp throughout, so "no loss" means "no detectable loss at n=50",
not "< 1 pp".

Findings:

1. Up to k=20 (17% of transformer compute in this geometry) aligned reuse has no
   detectable quality loss; at k=32 the CI excludes gains ([-10.3, 0.0]).
2. The step-0 (all-masked canvas) snapshot is the best single-snapshot source: no
   detectable loss at any k, including k=32 (28% saved). The last-step snapshot is the
   worst: it collapses for k >= 20 (irrelevant_append -30 pp at k=32). Prefix states
   recorded with a decoded old answer in the canvas carry that answer; states recorded
   against an all-mask canvas do not. This is a diffusion-specific effect and removes the
   need to store whole trajectories.
3. Per delta: multiturn_append is the most robust (0 to -2 pp at every k, answer agreement
   0.88-0.96). irrelevant_append is flat. hint_append loses 10-16 pp for k >= 16 with
   aligned/final and 8-12 pp with first: when D carries the answer, the shared-prefix
   representations that would have absorbed it are the ones being frozen. name_edit shows
   +8 to +20 pp (n=24; treat as noise until replicated).
4. Answer agreement with full recompute falls to 0.76-0.82 at k >= 16 even where accuracy
   is unchanged: reuse changes *which* answer is produced in about one in five prompts,
   with correct->wrong and wrong->correct flips balancing.

## Exp5: canvas resume keeps the old answer

| delta | t0=8 | t0=16 | t0=32 | t0=48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| irrelevant_append quality loss (pp) | 0.0 | 0.0 | -6.0 | -4.0 |
| hint_append quality loss (pp) | -6.0 | -8.0 | -18.0 | -24.0 |
| resumed answer == old answer (all deltas) | 0.46-0.88 | 0.64-0.88 | 0.82-0.96 | 0.92-0.96 |
| number_edit: resumed answer == old answer | 0.46 | 0.64 | 0.82 | 0.94 |

At t0=48 the resumed output reproduces the old request's answer in 92-96% of prompts for
every delta, including number_edit where the correct answer has changed (full recompute
agrees with old only 16%). Resume quality therefore equals old-answer quality: harmless
when D is irrelevant, -24 pp when D carries information (hint), wrong when D changes the
answer. The n=5 "75% of steps saved for free" reading was an artifact of
answer-preserving deltas; Round 2's fresh-fewer-steps baseline and D_changing class are
required before any claim about resume.

## Verdict against preregistered thresholds

- GO (>= 30% saved, < 1 pp loss): not met. The 30% mark is reachable only at k=32 where
  aligned loses 5 pp; the first-source result at k=32 (28% saved, +0.6 pp) is within CI
  of zero but the CI cannot resolve 1 pp.
- MAYBE (10-30%, workload dependent): met. 14-17% saved with no detectable loss for
  aligned; up to 28% for the step-0 snapshot source. Loss is delta-dependent
  (hint_append is the failure mode).
- KILL conditions: not triggered for layer reuse. Triggered for distance-based
  invalidation.

Decision: MAYBE / CONTINUE, as set on 2026-09-09. Priority order stays
canvas resume > layer-state reuse > distance (closed), with the caveat that resume now
carries a quantified risk (old-answer retention) that Round 2 H3/H5 must resolve.

## Positioning vs BiCache

BiCache: identical shared prefix, shallow-layer KV reuse is safe. Here: incrementally
changed context, reuse through layer 20 of 32 is quality-neutral for preserving deltas,
the safe depth depends on the semantic role of D rather than on its length or distance,
and the best cached state is the all-mask step-0 prefix state rather than any
step-aligned or final state.

## What carries into Round 2 unchanged

Sampler, model, gen 128 / 64 steps / block 32, primary metrics, bootstrap-CI reporting,
ROUND2_PREREG.md decision rules. New in Round 2 (already frozen): fresh-fewer-steps
baseline, shared-context sweep, explicit D_preserving vs D_changing, H5 gate observable.
One addition to record here before Round 2 starts: the step-0 snapshot source should be
the default layer-reuse source in Round 2, since it dominates aligned in both quality and
storage cost.
